DollarTicks reads public Deriv tick data and calculates last-digit frequencies locally.

🟢 Green half-circle = most occurring digit.
🔴 Red half-circle = least occurring digit.
🟣 Purple pointer = current moving tick.

Percentages are based on recent ticks and are not guaranteed predictions of the next tick.

The BUY button requests a fresh Deriv proposal and purchases the contract automatically.

CONTRACT STATUS checks the purchased contract and displays the final WIN/LOSS result.

TRADE HISTORY records completed trades on this device.
